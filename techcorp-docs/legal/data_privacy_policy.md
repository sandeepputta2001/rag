# Data Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Owner:** Legal & Compliance
**Review Cycle:** Annual

---

## Overview

TechCorp is committed to protecting the privacy of our customers, employees, and partners. This policy describes how TechCorp collects, processes, stores, and transfers personal data in compliance with applicable privacy regulations including GDPR, CCPA, and PIPEDA.

---

## Data We Collect

### Customer Data
| Category | Examples | Legal Basis |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| Account information | Name, email, company, billing address | Contract performance |
| Usage data | Feature usage, login timestamps, IP addresses | Legitimate interest |
| Support data | Ticket content, chat transcripts | Contract performance |
| Payment data | Last 4 digits, billing zip (full card data handled by Stripe) | Contract performance |

### Employee Data
- Personal identification: name, address, national ID, date of birth.
- Employment records: salary, performance reviews, leave records.
- IT activity logs: access logs for company systems (retained 12 months).

---

## Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Customer account data | Duration of contract + 3 years |
| Support tickets | 3 years |
| Billing records | 7 years (tax compliance) |
| Employee records | Duration of employment + 7 years |
| Security logs | 12 months |
| Marketing analytics | 2 years |

Data is securely deleted after the retention period using NIST SP 800-88 guidelines.

---

## Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Customers and employees have the right to:
- **Access** — request a copy of all personal data TechCorp holds about you.
- **Rectification** — request correction of inaccurate data.
- **Erasure** — request deletion of data (subject to legal retention obligations).
- **Portability** — receive your data in a machine-readable format.
- **Opt-out of sale** — TechCorp does not sell personal data.
- **Restrict processing** — in certain circumstances, request that processing be paused.

Submit requests at: privacy.techcorp.com/request
Response time: within 30 days (extended to 60 days for complex requests with notice).

---

## Data Transfers

- Data processed within the US and EU regions by default.
- Cross-border transfers to non-EU countries are protected by **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)**.
- Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) are available for enterprise customers on request.

---

## Security Measures

- Data at rest: AES-256 encryption.
- Data in transit: TLS 1.3.
- Access control: role-based, least-privilege.
- Annual third-party penetration testing.
- SOC 2 Type II report available under NDA.

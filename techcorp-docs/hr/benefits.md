# Employee Benefits Overview

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Owner:** Total Rewards, People Operations
**Review Cycle:** Annual (open enrollment each November)

---

## Health & Wellness

### Medical Insurance

TechCorp partners with BlueCross BlueShield to offer three medical plan tiers:

| Plan | Monthly Premium (Employee) | Deductible | Out-of-Pocket Max |
|------|---------------------------|------------|-------------------|
| Basic PPO | $0 | $2,000 | $6,000 |
| Premium PPO | $85 | $750 | $3,000 |
| HDHP + HSA | $0 | $1,500 | $4,500 |

- Coverage begins on the **first day of employment**.
- Dependents (spouse, domestic partner, children up to age 26) can be added during open enrollment or within 30 days of a qualifying life event.
- TechCorp contributes $500/year to HSA accounts for employees on the HDHP plan.

### Dental Insurance

- Preventive care (cleanings, X-rays): **100% covered**.
- Basic restorative (fillings): **80% covered** after deductible.
- Major restorative (crowns, root canals): **50% covered** after deductible.
- Orthodontia: **50% covered** up to a $2,000 lifetime maximum.
- Annual maximum benefit: $2,500 per person.

### Vision Insurance

- Eye exam: **$10 copay** (one per year).
- Frames or contacts: **$200 allowance** per year.
- Laser eye surgery discount: 15% off at participating providers.

### Mental Health

- 12 free therapy sessions per year through **Lyra Health**.
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP): 24/7 counseling hotline, financial coaching, legal consultations — all free and confidential.

### Wellness Stipend

$1,200 per year for eligible wellness expenses:
- Gym memberships, fitness classes, home workout equipment
- Meditation apps (Calm, Headspace)
- Ergonomic home office equipment (in addition to the equipment allowance)

## Retirement

### 401(k) Plan

- TechCorp matches **100% of contributions up to 6%** of base salary.
- Match vests **immediately** — there is no cliff or graded vesting schedule.
- Employees may contribute up to the IRS annual limit ($23,000 for 2024; $30,500 if age 50+).
- Roth 401(k) option available.
- Investment options managed through Fidelity; target-date funds and self-directed portfolios available.

## Time Off

### Paid Time Off (PTO)

| Tenure | PTO Days per Year |
|--------|-------------------|
| Year 1 | 15 days |
| Years 2–4 | 20 days |
| Year 5+ | Unlimited PTO |

Unlimited PTO employees are expected to take a **minimum of 15 days per year**. Manager approval required for absences longer than 5 consecutive days.

### Holidays

10 company-wide paid holidays per year, plus 2 **floating holidays** that can be used for any cultural, religious, or personal observance.

### Parental Leave

- **Primary caregiver:** 20 weeks paid leave.
- **Secondary caregiver:** 10 weeks paid leave.
- Available to all parents regardless of gender, including adoptive and foster parents.
- Leave can be taken continuously or intermittently within the first 12 months of the child's arrival.

### Sick Leave

- 10 days per year, non-accruing, non-rollover.
- No doctor's note required for absences up to 3 consecutive days.

### Bereavement Leave

- Immediate family: 5 days paid.
- Extended family / close friend: 3 days paid.
- Pregnancy loss: 5 days paid.

## Professional Development

### Learning Budget

- **$2,000 per employee per year**.
- Eligible expenses: online courses, conferences, certifications, books, workshops.
- Submit receipts through Expensify tagged "Learning & Development."
- Unused budget does not roll over.

### Platforms

TechCorp provides company-paid access to:
- **Coursera for Business** — unlimited access to 7,000+ courses.
- **LinkedIn Learning** — unlimited access.
- **O'Reilly Online** — technical books and video courses.

### Certification Reimbursement

Cloud and engineering certifications (AWS, GCP, Azure, CKA, etc.) are reimbursed **100%**, separate from the $2,000 learning budget.

# Remote Work Policy

**Effective Date:** March 1, 2024
**Owner:** People Operations
**Review Cycle:** Semi-Annual

---

## Overview

TechCorp supports a flexible hybrid work model that empowers employees to do their best work while maintaining meaningful in-person collaboration. This policy applies to all full-time employees unless their role is designated as on-site only.

---

## Hybrid Schedule

| Work Mode | Days per Week | Notes |
|-----------|--------------|-------|
| Remote | Up to 3 days | Employee's choice of days |
| In-Office | Minimum 2 days | Tuesday and Thursday are recommended anchor days |
| Full Remote (approved) | 5 days | Requires VP approval and annual review |

Teams may set specific anchor days. Check with your manager for team-level expectations.

---

## Core Hours

All employees, regardless of location, must be available during **core hours**:

- **10:00 AM – 3:00 PM** in the employee's local timezone.
- During core hours, employees must respond to messages within 30 minutes.
- Block time outside core hours is protected for deep work.

---

## Meeting Standards

- All recurring meetings must be **recorded** and uploaded to the team's shared drive within 2 hours of the meeting ending.
- Meeting recordings are retained for 90 days.
- Async-first: use Loom or written summaries for updates that do not require live discussion.
- Cameras are encouraged but not required during video calls.

---

## Technical Requirements

Remote employees are responsible for maintaining:

1. **Internet connection** — minimum 25 Mbps download / 10 Mbps upload.
2. **VPN** — mandatory when accessing any internal systems (Jira, Confluence, internal APIs). Use the TechCorp GlobalConnect VPN client.
3. **Quiet workspace** — a distraction-free environment during core hours and client-facing calls.
4. **Security** — full-disk encryption enabled on all work devices. Screen lock after 2 minutes of inactivity.

---

## Equipment & Stipends

| Item | Allowance | Frequency |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| Home office setup | $500 | One-time (on joining) |
| Internet reimbursement | $50/month | Monthly via expense report |
| Ergonomic accessories | $200 | Every 2 years |
| Monitor (if needed) | Company-issued | Submit request via IT portal |

Submit expense reports through Expensify with receipts within 30 days of purchase.

---

## Travel & In-Office Expectations

- Employees based more than 50 miles from a TechCorp office are **full-remote eligible**.
- Full-remote employees are expected to travel to HQ at least **once per quarter** for team sprints and all-hands events. Travel is fully expensed.
- Local employees who are consistently absent without prior approval may be moved to a required in-office schedule.

---

## Policy Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy (e.g., short-term full-remote arrangements during relocation, medical circumstances) require written approval from the employee's direct manager and People Operations. Approved exceptions are valid for a maximum of 90 days before re-evaluation.

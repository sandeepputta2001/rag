# Code of Conduct

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Owner:** Legal & Compliance, People Operations
**Review Cycle:** Annual

---

## Our Commitment

TechCorp is committed to providing a workplace where every employee is treated with dignity and respect. This Code of Conduct sets the baseline expectations for professional behavior across all TechCorp offices, remote environments, and company-sponsored events.

---

## Core Principles

1. **Integrity** — Be honest in all communications, both internal and with customers and partners. Never misrepresent facts, hide material information, or engage in deceptive practices.
2. **Respect** — Treat every colleague, customer, and partner with courtesy. Disagreements are resolved through constructive dialogue, not personal attacks.
3. **Inclusion** — TechCorp does not tolerate discrimination or harassment based on race, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, disability, religion, national origin, or any other protected characteristic.
4. **Accountability** — Own your mistakes. Raise concerns early. Do not cover up errors or shift blame.
5. **Confidentiality** — Protect TechCorp's proprietary information and customer data at all times.

---

## Anti-Harassment Policy

Harassment includes, but is not limited to:
- Unwelcome comments about appearance, identity, or personal life.
- Sharing or displaying offensive images, jokes, or content.
- Repeated unwanted contact (in-person, digital, or by phone).
- Threats, intimidation, or retaliation against someone who raises a concern.

**All reports are treated confidentially.** Retaliation against anyone who files a good-faith report is a serious violation and grounds for immediate termination.

---

## Conflict of Interest

Employees must disclose any personal financial interest in a TechCorp vendor, customer, or competitor to the Legal team. This includes:
- Ownership stakes above 1% in a competing company.
- A family member employed by a major vendor or partner.
- Accepting gifts valued above $50 from a vendor or customer.

---

## Use of Company Resources

- Company devices, networks, and software licenses are for business use. Limited personal use is acceptable as long as it does not interfere with work or violate any other policy.
- Employees may not use company infrastructure to mine cryptocurrency, host personal projects, or engage in any revenue-generating activity outside TechCorp.
- All work product created on company time or with company resources is the intellectual property of TechCorp.

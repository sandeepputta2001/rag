# TechCorp Pet Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Owner:** People Operations
**Review Cycle:** Annual

---

## Overview

TechCorp welcomes well-behaved pets in the office every Friday, affectionately called **Furry Fridays**. This policy outlines the rules, responsibilities, and procedures for bringing pets to TechCorp offices.

---

## Eligibility

- All full-time and part-time employees may participate.
- Pets must be pre-registered with People Operations at least 48 hours in advance.
- A maximum of 15 pets are permitted in the office on any given Friday to ensure a comfortable environment.

---

## Approved Animals

| Animal | Requirements |
|--------|-------------|
| Dogs | Must be leashed at all times in common areas |
| Cats | Must remain in an approved soft-sided carrier unless in designated pet zones |
| Small caged animals | Cage must remain closed (rabbits, guinea pigs, hamsters) |
| Birds | Must remain in a cage at all times |

Exotic animals, reptiles, and animals with a history of aggression are **not permitted**.

---

## Requirements for Dogs

1. Up-to-date on all core vaccinations: Rabies, DHPP, Bordetella.
2. Proof of vaccination must be on file with People Operations.
3. Must be on a leash no longer than 6 feet in corridors and common areas.
4. Must be house-trained and not exhibit excessive barking.
5. Dogs with a bite history require a behaviorist certificate of temperament.

---

## Designated Pet Areas

- **Floor 2, East Wing** — Primary pet-friendly workspace. Dog beds and water bowls provided.
- **Rooftop Garden** — Off-leash area available 12 PM – 1 PM (dogs only, max 5 at a time).
- **Lobby** — Transit only; pets must pass through quickly.

Pets are **not permitted** in the cafeteria, server rooms, executive floor (Floor 8), or any medical/wellness rooms.

---

## Office Mascot

The CEO's golden retriever, **Buddy**, serves as TechCorp's official office mascot. Buddy attends most Fridays and can be found near the executive lounge. Employees are welcome to greet Buddy but should ask permission before offering treats.

---

## Owner Responsibilities

- Owners are liable for any property damage or injuries caused by their pet.
- Clean up after your pet immediately. Pet waste bags are provided at all entry points.
- If a pet causes disruption or distress to other employees, the owner must remove the pet immediately.
- Notify People Ops of any pet-related incidents on the same day.

---

## Allergy & Phobia Accommodations

Employees with pet allergies or phobias may request a **pet-free workspace designation** by contacting People Operations. Designated pet-free floors are maintained every Friday.

Contact: people-ops@techcorp.internal | Extension: 4200

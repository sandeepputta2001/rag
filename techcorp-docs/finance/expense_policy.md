# Expense & Reimbursement Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Owner:** Finance
**Review Cycle:** Annual

---

## Overview

This policy governs how TechCorp employees submit, approve, and receive reimbursement for business expenses. All expenses must be necessary, reasonable, and incurred in the course of legitimate TechCorp business.

---

## Expense Categories & Limits

### Travel

| Expense | Limit | Notes |
|---------|-------|-------|
| Flights | Economy class | Business class permitted for flights > 6 hours with VP approval |
| Hotel | $250/night (US), $300/night (International) | Book through TripActions for preferred rates |
| Ground transport | Actual cost | Uber/Lyft acceptable; personal car at $0.67/mile |
| Meals (travel days) | $75/day | Alcohol not reimbursable without manager approval |
| International roaming | $50/day | Use TechCorp international SIM or Wi-Fi calling instead |

Book flights and hotels at least 14 days in advance whenever possible.

### Meals & Entertainment

| Expense | Limit | Approval Required |
|---------|-------|------------------|
| Team lunch / dinner | $50/person | Manager |
| Client entertainment | $100/person | Director |
| Team celebration | $75/person | VP (for events > 10 people) |
| Alcohol at team events | Included in meal limit | Manager |

All meals must list attendees and business purpose in the expense note.

### Office Supplies & Equipment

- Items under $50: reimbursed without prior approval.
- Items $50–$500: manager approval required.
- Items over $500: submit a Purchase Request via the Finance portal.

### Software & Subscriptions

- Individual SaaS tools must be approved by IT and Security before purchase.
- Submit software requests at it.techcorp.internal/software-request.
- Expense only after written IT approval is attached to the expense report.

### Learning & Development

- Up to $2,000/year (see Benefits policy for details).
- Submit via Expensify tagged "Learning & Development" with proof of completion where applicable.

---

## Submission Process

1. **Collect receipts** for all expenses over $25. Receipts are required regardless of amount for international expenses.
2. **Submit via Expensify** within **30 days** of the expense. Late submissions (>60 days) will not be reimbursed without CFO approval.
3. **Attach approvals** (email or Slack screenshot) for any expense requiring prior approval.
4. **Manager reviews and approves** in Expensify within 5 business days.
5. **Finance processes** approved reports every Friday. Payment via direct deposit within 5–7 business days.

---

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following will not be reimbursed under any circumstances:
- Personal travel, personal meals (non-business-related).
- Traffic fines, parking tickets.
- Alcohol beyond the allowances stated above.
- Purchases for personal use or items kept after employment ends.
- Expenses from a terminated employee after their last day.
- Charitable donations on behalf of TechCorp without prior Finance approval.

---

## Corporate Cards

Senior managers (L5+) and frequent travelers are eligible for a TechCorp Amex corporate card:
- Monthly statement due within 10 days of cycle close.
- Corporate card expenses still require receipts and notes in Expensify.
- Misuse of a corporate card is grounds for immediate termination.

Apply through: finance@techcorp.internal | Extension: 4300

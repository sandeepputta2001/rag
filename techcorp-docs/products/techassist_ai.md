## Overview

TechAssist AI is TechCorp's conversational AI platform for customer and employee support automation. Powered by large language models and trained on your organization's knowledge base, TechAssist AI deflects repetitive tickets, routes complex issues to the right agents, and delivers consistent, accurate answers at scale.

---

## Core Capabilities

### Conversational AI
- Natural language understanding in **45 languages**.
- Context-aware multi-turn conversations — remembers the full conversation history.
- Tone detection and adaptive response style (formal vs. casual).
- Handles clarifying questions and ambiguous intent gracefully.
- Escalation triggers when confidence score drops below a configurable threshold.

### Ticket Deflection & Resolution
- Average response time: **< 2 seconds**.
- Automated resolution rate: **78%** without human intervention (industry average: 40%).
- Suggested responses for agents handling escalated tickets.
- Auto-categorisation and priority tagging of incoming tickets.

### Knowledge Base Training
- Train on your internal documentation, FAQs, and past ticket resolutions.
- Supports PDF, DOCX, Confluence pages, Notion, and plain text.
- Continuous learning — the model improves as agents rate responses and provide corrections.
- Knowledge gap detection — flags questions the AI cannot confidently answer.

### Integrations
| Platform | Capability |
|----------|-----------|
| Zendesk | Bi-directional ticket sync, agent assist, CSAT sync |
| Jira Service Management | Issue creation, SLA tracking |
| ServiceNow | Incident and change management |
| Salesforce Service Cloud | Case creation and customer data enrichment |
| Slack / Microsoft Teams | Internal helpdesk bot |
| Intercom | Live chat widget |

### Analytics Dashboard
- Real-time metrics: active conversations, deflection rate, CSAT, AHT.
- Weekly and monthly trend reports (exportable to CSV, PDF).
- Per-topic breakdown — see which categories have the highest volume.
- Agent performance benchmarking.
- Anomaly detection — alerts when deflection rate drops significantly.

## Onboarding & Setup

1. **Connect your helpdesk** — OAuth integration takes under 5 minutes.
2. **Upload your knowledge base** — drag and drop documents or connect Confluence/Notion.
3. **Train and review** — run 50 test queries in the sandbox environment.
4. **Go live** — flip the switch; the AI starts handling tickets immediately.
5. **Tune** — use the dashboard to review low-confidence responses and improve accuracy.

Typical time-to-value: **2 weeks** from signed contract to live deployment.
